Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Mirkuk News Media Group ("Mirkuk News," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share when visiting our website. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We practice minimal data collection. When you browse Mirkuk News, we do not require account registration to read articles. We may collect non-identifying technical data such as browser type, device category, general geographic region, and referring pages to maintain site security and measure readership performance. If you contact our editorial desk directly via email, we retain your email address and message solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

Mirkuk News uses browser localStorage to remember your display preferences—specifically whether you have enabled Dark Mode—so that your reading experience remains consistent across visits. Essential session cookies may also be used to ensure fast page delivery and protect against automated abuse. You can clear or disable cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings.

3. Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade reader personal information to data brokers. If we display syndicated content, analytics tools, or advertising placements from vetted third-party partners, those partners operate under their own privacy policies and applicable data-protection laws. External links within articles lead to third-party websites whose privacy practices are outside our control.

4. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, wish to request access to or deletion of correspondence you have sent us, or have data-protection concerns, please contact our Data Compliance Officer:

  • Email: privacy@mirkuknews.com
  • Mailing Address: Privacy Office, Mirkuk News Media Group, 450 Lexington Ave, Suite 1800, New York, NY 10017
